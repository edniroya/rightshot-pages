What's new

The Mac app and the browser extension have their own version numbers. Newest first.

Browser extension 0.5.0

November 2026

  • Works on any computer, with or without the Mac app. Each capture opens in a tab of its own, already copied, with Copy and Download.
  • Capture Section: point at a table, a chart or any part of a page and click. Taller than the screen is fine. Press the up arrow for the bigger section around it.
  • Capture Area: drag over the part you want. Hold the pointer at the bottom edge to keep going down the page.
  • Capture Visible Page: what's on screen, without the browser's tabs and address bar.
  • Right-click any page and choose RightShot for the same captures.
  • Keyboard shortcuts: Option+Shift (Alt+Shift on Windows) with P, E, A or V. Change them from the RightShot menu.
  • Full pages leave out cookie banners and chat bubbles, and bars that stay on screen appear once.
  • Captures keep their real size on sharp screens.
  • With RightShot for Mac: Capture Scrolling Area (Pro). Drag over a chat or a long list on the page, scroll, and get one tall image, just as on the Mac.

RightShot for Mac 1.0

First release, coming soon to the Mac App Store

  • Right-drag over anything to capture it. Release, and it's on your clipboard with editing tools right below it. A quick right-click still opens the usual menu.
  • Hold the right button for a menu with every capture: area, window, full screen, previous area and text.
  • Copy the text from anything on screen, free.
  • The shelf: your last 30 captures along the top of the screen. Click to copy, drag into any app, pin the ones you need again.
  • Mark up where you took it: boxes, arrows, numbered steps, text, highlighter, blur and redaction.
  • Record the screen, free: an area, a window or the whole screen, with system audio and your microphone.
  • RightShot Pro, free for 14 days, then a one-time purchase: Scrolling Capture, Recording Studio, your camera in recordings, Save as GIF, Create Guide, Studio, Auto Redact and shelf search.
