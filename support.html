Support

Answers to common questions, and how to reach us.

Email hello@edniroya.comTell us your macOS version and what you expected to happen. We usually reply within two working days.
Right-drag doesn't start a capture

Open RightShot's menu and choose Right-Drag Capture so it's ticked. Then allow RightShot in System Settings, Privacy and Security, under both Accessibility and Screen and System Audio Recording. If RightShot isn't in the Accessibility list, click the plus button and add it from your Applications folder. RightShot notices within a second.

My normal right-click menu stopped appearing

A quick right-click should always reach the app you're in. If you're holding the button still for half a second, RightShot opens its own menu instead. For apps that use right-drag themselves, such as 3D tools, games and remote desktops, open Settings, Capture and click Add App under Leave right-drag alone in. Or choose a key under Start right-drag, so RightShot only starts while you hold that key.

I'm on a trackpad

On supported trackpads, press with two fingers and drag. You can also choose Capture Area from the menu bar and drag with one finger, which only needs Screen and System Audio Recording.

Where are my screenshots saved?

Every capture is copied to the clipboard. With Auto-Save on, it's also saved to Pictures/RightShot by default. To save to the Desktop or another folder, choose it in Settings, Saving, and click Allow when macOS asks, so RightShot may save there. With Auto-Save off, recordings go to Movies/RightShot.

The browser extension can't reach RightShot

Open RightShot first, then try again. If it still can't connect, choose Settings, General, Reconnect Browser Extension, then quit and reopen the browser. To capture pages opened from a file, turn on Allow access to file URLs in the extension's details.

Recording has no sound

Audio is off until you turn it on. In Settings, Recording, turn on System audio, Microphone, or both. The first time you turn on the microphone, macOS asks for permission.

How do I restore RightShot Pro on another Mac?

Sign in to the Mac App Store with the same Apple Account, open any Pro tool in RightShot and choose Restore Purchases.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases go through Apple, so refunds do too. Use Apple's Report a Problem page and choose RightShot Pro.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything stays on your Mac. RightShot has no account, no analytics and no server. Read the privacy policy for the details.
