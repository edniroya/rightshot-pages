Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026 · Version 1.0

In one sentence: RightShot keeps everything you capture on your Mac. There is no RightShot account, no analytics, no tracking and no RightShot server, so we never see your screenshots, recordings or anything in them.

1. Who we are

RightShot is made by Edniroya Ltd ("we", "us"), registered in England and Wales, company number 09367944. Edniroya Ltd is the data controller for the little personal data described here. Contact us at hello@edniroya.com.

2. What stays on your Mac

Everything RightShot makes or reads stays on your Mac, in places you control:

  • Screenshots, recordings, GIFs, Studio images and guides you save.
  • What RightShot puts on your clipboard.
  • Your settings, such as your Auto-Save folder, recording options and the apps where right-drag is left alone.
  • Access to folders you pick for saving, which macOS keeps so RightShot can save there again.

We have no copy of any of it. If you delete a file or uninstall RightShot, we cannot recover anything for you.

3. Processing done on your Mac

Copy Text reads text in a capture, and Auto Redact looks for faces, email addresses, phone numbers and account or card numbers. Both use the text and face recognition built into macOS and run entirely on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded, and the results are not stored anywhere except the capture you choose to keep.

4. Permissions RightShot asks for

PermissionWhy
Screen and System Audio RecordingTo capture what's on screen, and to record the screen and system audio when you choose to.
AccessibilityTo tell a right-drag apart from a normal right-click, so capture can start from the right mouse button. RightShot doesn't read or control other apps' content with it.
MicrophoneOnly when you turn on Microphone for recordings.
Folders you chooseTo save captures to the folder you pick. Pictures/RightShot is the default.

You can turn any of these off in System Settings at any time.

5. The browser extension

The optional RightShot extension for Chrome, Brave and Edge captures a whole page or records a tab when you click it. It works only on the tab you clicked it in, only at that moment, and sends the capture straight to the RightShot app on the same Mac. Without the RightShot app, a capture passes through the extension's own storage on your computer only until its tab shows it, and is never sent anywhere. It does not collect your browsing history, does not run on other pages, and does not send anything to any server.

6. Purchases

The free trial and the RightShot Pro unlock are handled by Apple through the Mac App Store. Apple processes your payment and Apple Account under Apple's own privacy policy. RightShot only checks, on your Mac, whether your Apple Account has started the trial or bought Pro. Apple gives us sales reports that don't identify you.

7. This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses fonts from Google Fonts. Like any web host, GitHub and Google may log your IP address and browser details to deliver and protect their services. The site sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

8. If you contact us

If you email us, we use your email address and what you send only to reply and to fix the problem you report. We keep support emails for up to two years, then delete them. Never send us screenshots that contain anything you don't want us to see.

9. Children

RightShot is a general productivity app and doesn't knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

10. Your rights

Under UK and EU data protection law you can ask us for a copy of personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. In practice, the only data we could hold is a support email. Write to hello@edniroya.com. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here with a new date and version number. If a change affects what leaves your Mac, we'll say so in the app's release notes too.
